# Awesome-Public-Incident-Reporting

## Top Incident Reporting (Public Safety) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Internal Affairs, Use-of-Force Reporting, Early Intervention, Case Management & Public Safety Accountability*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Incident Reporting in Public Safety**. These systems help law enforcement and public safety agencies capture, investigate, track, and analyze incidents such as use of force, complaints, internal affairs cases, and related events for accountability, early intervention, and compliance.



**Examples** include IAPro, Guardian Tracking, Vector Solutions Guardian Tracking, Case IQ, Resolver, Origami Risk, SafeCities, PowerDMS, Benchmark Gensuite, and Intelex (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Specialized public-safety incident and internal-affairs platforms are almost entirely commercial. Open options exist mainly as general case/incident management tools, open data projects, and experimental systems. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[IAPro](https://www.ci-technologies.com/)**  

  Widely used internal affairs and professional standards software for law enforcement incident tracking, investigations, and early intervention.



- **[Guardian Tracking](https://www.guardiantracking.com/)**  

  Performance and incident tracking platform used by public safety agencies for employee performance, counseling, and related documentation.



- **[Vector Solutions Guardian Tracking](https://www.vectorsolutions.com/)**  

  Guardian Tracking capabilities offered within the Vector Solutions public safety and training ecosystem.



- **[Case IQ](https://www.caseiq.com/)**  

  Incident reporting and investigative case management platform used for ethics, compliance, workplace, and related investigations (also applied in some public-sector contexts).



- **[Resolver](https://www.resolver.com/)**  

  Risk and incident management platform supporting investigation workflows and organizational risk processes.



- **[Origami Risk](https://www.origamirisk.com/)**  

  Risk management and incident reporting platform used across various industries including public sector risk programs.



- **[SafeCities](https://www.safecities.com/)** (or similar public safety platforms)  

  Technology solutions supporting public safety operations and related reporting needs.



- **[PowerDMS](https://www.powerdms.com/)**  

  Policy management and training platform frequently used by public safety agencies alongside incident and compliance processes.



- **[Benchmark Gensuite](https://www.benchmarkgensuite.com/)**  

  EHS and operational risk platform that includes incident management capabilities applicable to broader organizational settings.



- **[Intelex](https://www.intelex.com/)**  

  EHSQ and risk platform offering incident reporting and investigation workflows used in various regulated environments.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OpenPoliceData and related open data projects](https://github.com/openpolicedata/openpolicedata)**  

  Open libraries and tools that provide centralized access to incident-level police data (use of force, complaints, stops, etc.) for analysis and transparency.



- **[Comport and accountability open tools](https://github.com/codeforamerica/comport)**  

  Projects aimed at helping agencies open internal-affairs style data and improve public accountability (historical Code for America work).



- **[General open incident and case management systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source case management, ticketing, or incident tracking platforms that can be adapted for basic public-safety reporting needs.



- **[Incident command and emergency management open platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Open systems (including descendants of NICS and related projects) focused on operational incident coordination rather than internal affairs.



- **[Policy and training open document systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open tools for managing policies and training records that often complement incident reporting programs.



- **[Early intervention and analytics open experiments](https://github.com/)**  

  Research and prototype tools for analyzing patterns in incident data that may support early intervention concepts.



- **[Form and intake open builders](https://github.com/)**  

  Open form platforms that can be configured for citizen or internal incident reporting intake.



- **[Audit logging and chain-of-custody open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Components useful for maintaining secure records of investigative actions.



- **[Data visualization and dashboard open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open BI and dashboard projects used to present incident statistics and trends.



- **[Integration and export open utilities](https://github.com/)**  

  Scripts for moving data between records management systems, IA platforms, and public reporting portals.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using open data libraries (such as OpenPoliceData) for analysis and transparency work rather than operational case management.

- Adapting general open case management or ticketing systems for lighter internal tracking needs.

- Combining open policy/document tools with commercial incident platforms.

- Accepting that specialized internal affairs workflows, early intervention systems, secure investigation case files, and agency-specific compliance features still require commercial platforms (IAPro, Guardian Tracking, Case IQ, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency, analytics, and public reporting rather than replacing core investigative systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture incidents via open forms or existing RMS → store records in a secure open or commercial case system → apply open analytics for pattern detection → publish appropriate public data via open portals. Suitable mainly for transparency and analysis use cases. Operational internal-affairs and professional standards programs in most agencies continue to rely on proven commercial platforms designed for the unique requirements of public safety investigations.
